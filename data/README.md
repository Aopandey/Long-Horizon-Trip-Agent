# Synthetic dataset

Generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py` (seed 5130), in the format in [docs/DATA_FORMAT.md](../docs/DATA_FORMAT.md). All names and values are invented, for controlled experiments only.

```bash
python3 scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py   # rebuilds data/ (same seed = identical files)
trip-agent validate data/                    # 80 scenarios, dataset OK, hash f3270941decf0d9a
```

## Catalog
- **Places:** 8 Boston neighborhoods with 40 attractions, 24 restaurants and 8 hotels.
- **Tight attractions:** 12 attractions have tight opening hours, with 2 hours or less of spare time beyond the visit.
- **Closed days:** many places are closed on one or two weekdays.
- **Travel:** 2,556 travel legs, one per pair of places. Each place gets a position near its neighborhood centre, and times come from distance:
  - up to 1.2 km is walked (free)
  - longer trips use transit ($2.40 per person)
  - crossing the river to Cambridge adds 6 minutes

## Scenarios
80 scenarios: 40 `dev` (for tuning) and 40 `eval` (for the final 720 runs), with 10 per horizon in each.

| Horizon | Days | Required attractions | Meals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 1 | 3 | lunch and dinner each day |
| 10 | 2 | 6 | lunch and dinner each day |
| 20 | 4 | 12 | lunch and dinner each day |
| 30 | 6 | 18 | lunch and dinner each day |

- **Same difficulty per day:** every day has 1 tight and 2 regular required attractions, at every horizon.
- **Random draws:** group size 1 to 4 and a start date in June to August 2026. About 30% of trips limit the hotel to 2 neighborhoods.
- **Reference plan:** each scenario has a hidden reference plan, the cheapest plan the search finds (it tries every visit order and the 8 nearest restaurants for each meal). The validator confirms every one is feasible under the true facts.
- **Budget:** each budget is 8 to 15% above the reference cost.
- **No repeats:** no two scenarios share the same set of required attractions.

`summary.csv` has one row per scenario: group, dates, hotel, cost, budget, slack and the number of random draws needed.
